Surge Rider — a pump.fun bonding-curve momentum bot for the make.fun Pump.fun Bot Sprint. It waits for a curve to prove real buy pressure (several distinct buyers, net SOL flowing in, price climbing but not parabolic), buys a small first tranche, pyramids only into strength, and exits on a stop loss, a trailing stop, fading inflow, a big single dump, or a time limit. A pace guard relaxes the entry bar and fires small late-run probes so the entry clears the 20-coin / 20-round-trip guardrail.
